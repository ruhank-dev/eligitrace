A 67-year-old man with type 2 diabetes mellitus diagnosed 12 years ago presents for routine follow-up. His most recent HbA1c was 8.4% despite metformin 1000 mg twice daily. He has stage 3 chronic kidney disease with an eGFR of 48 mL/min/1.73m2. He had a myocardial infarction four years ago treated with a stent. He quit smoking 20 years ago. He is not on insulin. He lives alone and is able to give informed consent.
